Each recipe has a distinct physical ingredient arrangement and recipe-book unlock advancement. Existing materials suffice; no filler material is introduced.

Items obtained by another system retain their acquisition route: spawn eggs are creative tools; Spectrums unlock at birth; spell/projectile/execution objects are abilities; priorite is a natural resource; immortal wheels/fingers/orbs are progression or world rewards; photos, exposed film and recorded VHS require real media operations; fish/meat/buckets come from fish and processing; flour/concentrates/tailings/crushed ores come from their installed working machines; plants/logs/apple saplings occur naturally; admin debug turrets and ban hammers stay administrative; placed media/Nexus pool blocks are internal world representations. Assembly bodies and working parts retain their existing staged manufacturing routes, never an automatic complete machine.

Verification uses the live dedicated-server recipe manager: vanilla 2×2 table recipe, all new survival recipes, ingredient registry resolution and unique matching of their representative grids. Further registry audit is recorded by the same test to catch overlooked craftable items.

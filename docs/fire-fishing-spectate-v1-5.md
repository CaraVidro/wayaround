# Fire, fishing and entity observation

This branch preserves Little Leaf World/Nexus/current environmental fixes and integrates the separate multiplayer configuration timeout commits without replacing the newer world systems.

Roots/branches and terrestrial mod plants expose native NeoForge fire-spread and consumption properties. Water-containing blocks remain protected. Heat converts grass surfaces to dirt while retaining the solid terrain and respecting doFireTick. Existing fire work budgets remain active. Clients receive up to 16 merged far wildfire footprints within 256 blocks, alongside the 128 nearby flame samples. Far flames are broad, low atlas meshes with depth testing; no additional flame entities. Only loaded, tracked active fires contribute, so unloaded fires are not invented or remotely simulated.

Normal fishing uses actual fish. The lure targets submerged space, has a larger acquisition/bite radius and shorter wait, and briefly owns fish movement so ecological schooling/feeding cannot overwrite the approach. Bites use the native synced hooked-entity state, visible splash and existing sounds. Click gives a tug. Holding use for five ticks starts steady reeling; releasing stops it, and a missing input refresh expires after 15 ticks. Once the hook reaches the player it is removed without creating loot; the real fish remains in the world. Magic rod behavior is preserved.

All current vanilla/mod fish gain a physical processing profile, including regional fish. Their corpses retain species, size, cooking and remaining biomass across saves and whole-body pickup/replacement. Meat and whole-body items are registered where missing; existing regional meat is reused. Skeletons yield bones; jellyfish yield none. Cetaceans retain their existing dedicated carcasses and Cleinton remains immortal. New silhouettes are simplified block meshes; food item icons use vanilla fish textures.

Entity Spectate is crafted from a spyglass, ender pearl and amethyst shard. Holding it hides the player/armor/hands; an entity interaction or attack starts observation without hurting the target. The camera follows any live clicked entity, including players, with mouse-controlled orbit and obstacle clipping. Another click, changing item, target death/dimension change or disconnect exits. The observer cannot attack, mine or collect items while attached, and has temporary protection/no collision; exiting restores the original location and flags. A saved interrupted visit is restored on login. No game-mode changes are used.

Abyss cleanup now scans below erroneous floating-decoration heightmap entries, repairs unsupported known floor decoration and periodically makes old loose kelp/pebbles/skulls sink without deleting stacks. Supported vegetation and unrelated loot are preserved. Searches stay within loaded chunks and use existing per-player edit budgets.

Automated checks cover native fuel properties, grass damage/game-rule protection, registered processing profiles, corpse biomass persistence, real hook attachment, observation entry/exit, loose-debris conservation and bounded wildfire frames. Manual in-world checks remain necessary for orbit feel, long fishing sessions, terrain-dependent flames and multiplayer observation.

## Follow-up: wildfire distance, observation and flowing water

- Wildfire detail changes with distance: nearby native individual flames, 8/16-block panels at medium distance, one growing silhouette per connected fire component far away. Actual flame positions within three blocks connect components; unrelated fires are never merged solely because the viewer is far away. Footprints and height are bounded (256 and 32 blocks), input at 1,024 positions and output at 16 patches; no remote chunk loading.
- Entity Spectate retains camera ownership on the local player and orbits the target visually, avoiding native camera travel and physical player interaction with the target. Holding the item grants spectator exclusion from collision/AI, flight, damage protection, effect rejection, invisibility and pickup suppression. Shift or another click exits the camera near its current viewpoint; ghost protection continues until the item is removed. Original body flags are saved/restored on item removal and disconnect. Selection packets still validate held item, live target, same dimension, six-block reach and line of sight.
- Generated river pebbles now explicitly accept both source and flowing water, scheduling normal fluid propagation and retaining the rock. Standard Minecraft waterlogging uses source water inside the rock cell. Full terrain blocks remain solid.
- Network protocol 27: update server and clients together.
- Automated coverage extends the observation test and adds flowing-water propagation and connected wildfire distance/height contracts. In-game visual and multiplayer movement QA remains necessary.
